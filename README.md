### Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition · Isle of Man

## 1. README

### What it is
A browser-based teaching and revision tool for GCSE Maths Intermediate Tier — giving teachers, tutors and students instant access to 140+ randomised question factories across the full Intermediate Tier curriculum, complete with full worked solutions and a built-in interactive whiteboard. Compatible with AQA, Edexcel, WJEC and IGCSE.

### Who it's for
- Maths teachers selecting and delivering targeted topic practice in class
- Tutors using it in lessons to teach a topic and then have students practise immediately
- Year 10 & 11 students targeting grades 6–9 who want to focus revision on specific topics
- Students preparing for A-Level Maths

### What's inside
**140+ question factories** covering Number, Algebra, Geometry and Statistics. Click any topic in the sidebar to generate a new randomised question instantly — or use the search bar to jump straight to a factory by name or question ID prefix.

- **Full worked solutions** revealed on demand — press Show Solution to see the full worked method
- **Built-in interactive whiteboard** — pen (3 sizes, colour picker), eraser, undo, and clear tools, with a choice of Plain, Lines, or Grid background, so you can draw, annotate and explain solutions live in lessons
- **Save your working** as a PNG at any time
- **Click to copy** any question or value straight from the panel
- **Students can work through questions directly on screen**, generating a fresh version with New Question whenever they need more practice on the same topic

### How to use it
Open the page in any browser — nothing to install. Choose a topic from the sidebar to generate a question, work through it on the whiteboard, then reveal the worked solution to check the method. Press New Question for a fresh, freshly randomised version of the same topic whenever you want more practice.

No app, subscription or account required. Fully private — nothing stored or tracked. Works in any browser on any device.

### After purchase
You'll receive a PDF with a single button — tap it to open the tool instantly in your browser on any device. Keep your purchase email safe as it contains your access link. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" in Safari to save it like an app.

### A note on exam boards
Compatible with AQA, Edexcel, WJEC and IGCSE — these describe the general style and level of a question, not certification against one current, dated specification. Always check your own board's up-to-date specification for definitive guidance.
